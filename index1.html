Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking so we can prepare for your visit and keep waiting times short. Urgent pain can be discussed by phone.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months. Your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your needs.

Is treatment painful?

We use local anaesthesia and gentle techniques to keep you comfortable. Tell us about any concerns and we will adjust the approach.

What services do you offer?

Examinations, professional cleaning, fillings, extractions, root canal treatment, dentures, crowns and bridges, porcelain teeth, braces, teeth whitening, and silver or gold teeth.

How do I prepare for my visit?

Bring any previous dental X-rays or records if you have them, and a list of any medication you take. Arrive a few minutes early to complete your details.
